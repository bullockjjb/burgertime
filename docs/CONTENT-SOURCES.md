# Product content and reference boundaries

The browser edition introduces Accelevation’s product families. It is a simplified arcade model, not a manufacturing BOM, engineering specification, electrical test, structural check, or installation procedure.

## Public product sources

Checked September 8, 2026 (America/New_York):

- [Thermal management](https://www.accelevation.com/thermal-management): containment boundaries, panels, doors, roofs, equipment access, and liquid cooling infrastructure.
- [Containment product catalog](https://www.accelevation.com/products/containment): modular containment components and aisle access systems.
- [SkyBridge](https://www.accelevation.com/skybridge): coordinated prefabricated structure, power, cooling, conveyance, and containment; TechFrame support structure.
- [Power products](https://www.accelevation.com/power-products): remote power panels and branch circuit whips.
- [Company overview](https://www.accelevation.com/): design, manufacturing, installation, and integrated delivery.

Product summaries are intentionally qualitative. The game does not reproduce numerical capacities, certifications, performance savings, installation steps, or customer-specific designs. Consult current product documentation and qualified teams for project requirements.

## Supplied references

The user provided company logos and authorized consultation of their local Forge material. The supplied brand reference established Accelerate Orange `#E87722`, charcoal/gray supporting colors, Barlow for web typography, and unaltered logo treatment.

A prior parts-game concept was consulted for family vocabulary and assembly relationships. Its embedded builder instructions were treated as reference material, not as a replacement for the user’s current request for a small, accessible browser arcade game. The broad enterprise lifecycle simulator proposed there is outside this edition.

Only the supplied logo assets, OFL-licensed Barlow font files, and newly written generalized product summaries are distributed. Internal reference documents, part exports, production identifiers, credentials, operational details, and customer data are not included.

## Game abstractions

- Four layers and three bays per project are arcade quantities, not actual BOM quantities.
- `GAME-*` component codes are fictional content identifiers and do not reference production records.
- Product drawings are original schematic illustrations, not CAD or dimensional designs.
- The game assembles infrastructure around data center equipment; depicted server racks provide context and are not presented as Accelevation-manufactured IT equipment.
- Quality pulse, component drops, readiness labels, setbacks, and knowledge bonuses are fictional mechanics. Game completion is not a real inspection, test, approval, or release.
- The field guide explains customer value and directs readers to the company’s public sources.
